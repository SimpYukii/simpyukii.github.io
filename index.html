SimpYukii Clips

A private publishing tool operated by SimpYukii.

SimpYukii Clips is a private tool that prepares short gameplay highlight videos and publishes them to the operator's own YouTube channel and TikTok account. It is used only by its operator; it does not offer accounts or services to other people.

Source material: public Twitch clips and streams of creators who allow clipping or take part in clipping programs. Every video credits the creator, and videos are removed immediately if a creator objects.
