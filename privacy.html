Privacy Policy

SimpYukii Clips · Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. Who we are

SimpYukii Clips ("the App") is a private tool operated by SimpYukii ("we"). Contact: vincent.muehlenbein2@gmail.com.

2. Who uses the App

The App is used solely by its operator to publish videos to the operator's own YouTube channel and TikTok account. It does not provide accounts or services to other users.

3. Data the App accesses

  • YouTube API Services: basic channel information (channel ID and name) and permission to upload videos and set their title, description and privacy status on the operator's channel.
  • TikTok: basic account information (open ID, display name) and permission to upload videos to the operator's account.
  • Twitch API and YouTube Data API: publicly available video metadata (titles, creator names, links, durations, view counts) used to find source material.

4. How the data is used

Data is used only to find source videos, prepare highlight clips, upload them to the operator's own accounts and credit the original creators. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties and do not use it for advertising or profiling.

5. Storage and security

Access tokens and video metadata are stored on a private server located in Germany (Hetzner Online GmbH), protected by key-based access and a firewall. Tokens are never published or shared.

6. Retention and deletion

Metadata of source videos is kept as long as needed to manage uploads and credits, at most 12 months. Tokens are deleted when access is revoked. You can revoke the App's access to a Google account at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions, and to a TikTok account in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. To request deletion of stored data, email us.

7. Google and YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Cookies

This website and the App do not use cookies or tracking.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact us at the address above.
